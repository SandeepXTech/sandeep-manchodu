03 · The dream

Financial freedom, please. 😌

I want to reach a point where buying something I like doesn't require a budget meeting with myself.

No calculator. No checking my bank balance seventeen times. No internal committee asking whether I really need it. Just making sensible choices without stressing over every little purchase.

I'd also love to build a life beyond the standard 9–5 routine. Not because I want to avoid hard work, but because I want more freedom over what I do, what I build, and how I spend my time.

The goal is to work towards a life I enjoy — with room for new experiences, meaningful work, and the occasional completely unnecessary purchase. 😂
