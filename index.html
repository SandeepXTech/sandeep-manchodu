The meaning behind the name

“Manchodu” — goodness and character.

In Telugu, related expressions include మంచివాడు (manchivāḍu), meaning “a good man,” and మంచి మనిషి (manchi manishi), meaning “a good person.” The exact sense depends on context. For my personal brand, I use “Manchodu” to express kindness, honesty, respect and good character.

Related ideas in other languages: English — good person, kind-hearted person, gentleman; Hindi — अच्छा इंसान (achchha insaan); Tamil — நல்ல மனிதர் (nalla manithar); Kannada — ಒಳ್ಳೆಯ ವ್ಯಕ್ತಿ (olleya vyakti); Malayalam — നല്ല മനുഷ്യൻ (nalla manushyan); Spanish — buena persona; French — personne bienveillante; German — guter Mensch; Japanese — いい人 (ii hito).
