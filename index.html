The bigger picture

Freedom is the real flex.

My dream? To become financially free enough that buying something I like doesn't require a calculator, three budget revisions and a meeting with my inner finance minister.

I don't dream of avoiding hard work. I dream of having more control over it — building something meaningful, exploring opportunities and having the freedom to enjoy the life I'm working for.

And the classic 9–5 routine? I'd rather create my own path if I can. Big dream, yes. Impossible? Let's see. 😎

The meaning behind the name

Manchodu — a name with meaning.

In Telugu, related expressions include మంచివాడు (manchivāḍu), meaning “a good man,” and మంచి మనిషి (manchi manishi), meaning “a good person.” The exact sense depends on context. For my personal brand, Manchodu represents kindness, honesty, respect and good character.
